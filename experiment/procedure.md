<b> Lab launching procedure : </b> <br>

1. Download the CRO experiment file from the provided link.
2. Install the APK file into your Oculus VR headset.
3. Launch the application from the device menu.
4. Adjust your VR headset for a comfortable fit and ensure the controllers are paired.


<b>Working of an experiment : </b> <br>

•	You can virtually “walk” around the lab environment and interact with the CRO and signal generator using VR controllers.<br>
•	Grab and put the resistor on the breadboard.<br>
•	Adjust the CRO’s amplitude control to change the vertical size of the displayed waveform.<br>
•	Observe different waveforms (sine, square, triangular) generated by the signal generator.
