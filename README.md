### About the Experiment 

Through an immersive setting, virtual reality (VR) is a technology tool that has the potential to enable people to live extraordinary experiences. With its entertaining dynamics, virtual reality (VR) is a cutting-edge educational tool that seeks to enhance teaching-learning approaches and increase students' focus and enthusiasm in studying. Its uses span from engineering to medical education, enabling it to practice and simulate in a variety of settings without endangering the physical integrity of the people or the equipment being utilized. This lab concerns about the function of logical gates which is a regular part of our curriculum. It emphasizes more about how various gates work and their logical concepts.
